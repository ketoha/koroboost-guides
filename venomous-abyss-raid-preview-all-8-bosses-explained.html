Venomous Abyss Raid Preview: All 8 Bosses Explained

A guide by Koroboost

The Venomous Abyss is Season 2's raid tier -- eight bosses deep, available in Normal, Heroic, and Mythic for 10-30 players. Set beneath the Coiled Isle in a sprawling underground serpent temple, this raid tells the story of Ula'tek's curse and the forces corrupted by it.

Here's every boss in order, what we know about each encounter, and what to expect when you step inside.

Boss 1: Nek'zali the Soulcoiler

The raid opener is a shadow hunter who guards the entrance to the deeper chambers. Nek'zali sets the tone for the entire raid -- expect shadow magic, soul-based mechanics, and a fight that teaches your group to spread and react to targeted abilities.

As the first boss, Nek'zali should be tuned as a gear check rather than a wall. Normal and Heroic shouldn't give organized groups much trouble, but Mythic will likely add overlapping mechanics that demand tighter execution. A solid warm-up before things get serious.

Boss 2: Entombed Sentinels

Corrupted golems that have been twisted by the venom seeping through the temple. This is a multi-target encounter -- you're fighting multiple sentinels either simultaneously or in sequence.

Multi-add fights tend to favor cleave-heavy compositions and require strong add priority management. Expect tank swaps between golems, shield or absorb mechanics that need to be broken, and environmental venom that limits your positioning. This is where your raid's coordination gets tested early.

Boss 3: Vashnik the Malignant

A venom distiller who's been brewing toxic concoctions deep in the abyss. Vashnik is likely a "mad scientist" style encounter with potion or cauldron mechanics -- think DoT management, debuff clearing, and possibly a phase where the room fills with hazardous zones.

These types of bosses tend to be healer-intensive. Your raid's ability to manage spreading damage while dealing with targeted mechanics will determine how smooth this goes. Position near the edge, keep dispels ready, and don't stand in the green stuff.

Boss 4: The Lost Explorers

This is one of the more thematically interesting encounters. The Lost Explorers are possessed tortollans who ventured too deep and fell under Ula'tek's influence. You're not fighting enemies so much as trying to deal with corrupted allies.

Expect a multi-phase or multi-target fight with mechanics tied to the tortollans' original nature twisted by the curse. There may be "save or kill" decision-making, crowd control elements, or phases where the explorers alternate between hostile and vulnerable states. These encounters tend to be puzzle-like and favor groups that adapt quickly.

Boss 5: Sszorak

A warped creature born from the abyss itself. Sszorak is pure corruption given physical form -- the kind of boss that exists because the environment itself has become hostile.

Mid-raid bosses like this often serve as DPS and healing checks before the final wing. Expect raw damage output, possibly enrage-style mechanics, and a fight that rewards clean rotations and cooldown management. Sszorak sits at the midpoint of the raid, so it should be tuned to gate undergeared groups while remaining manageable for those on progression pace.

Boss 6: The Twin Fangs -- Vexhul & Ithraz

A dual-boss encounter featuring a brood fighting in a sea of venom. The Twin Fangs -- Vexhul and Ithraz -- are likely a "fight two bosses with shared health or coordinated mechanics" style encounter.

Twin boss fights are historically some of WoW's best encounters. Expect split raid mechanics (one group on each boss), abilities that interact when the bosses are too close or too far apart, and a venom sea environmental hazard that limits movement space. This could easily be the hardest fight in the raid outside of the final two bosses, especially on Mythic.

Boss 7: The Coiled Altar

The penultimate encounter features Zul'jan under the influence of Malacrass. If those names ring a bell, they should -- this is a callback to troll raid history, recontextualized through the Midnight serpent curse.

The Coiled Altar fight likely involves Zul'jan channeling power from the altar while Malacrass's influence creates additional mechanics. Expect a multi-phase encounter with an intermission, add waves, and escalating damage as the altar's power grows. Penultimate bosses are traditionally tuned tight, so this will probably be a progression wall for many guilds on Heroic and Mythic.

Boss 8: Ula'tek

The final boss. The source of the curse. Ula'tek is the culmination of the entire Coiled Isle storyline and the Season 2 raid tier.

Final bosses in modern WoW follow a pattern: multiple phases, an intermission or transition, and a burn phase at the end. Ula'tek will almost certainly have at least three phases with escalating mechanics. The curse theme suggests stacking debuffs, corruption management, and possibly a "cleanse or succumb" mechanic that forces individual responsibility.

On Mythic, expect a completely different final phase with unique mechanics that don't exist on other difficulties. This is where the world-first race will be decided, and where Ahead of the Curve becomes the goal for the broader raiding community.

Raid Difficulty and Group Size

Quick reference:

  • Normal: 10-30 players, flex scaling. Good for learning fights and early gearing.
  • Heroic: 10-30 players, flex scaling. The target for most organized guilds. Drops Hero and Myth-track gear depending on the boss.
  • Mythic: 20 players, fixed size. The hardest content in the game with unique mechanics on every boss.

Preparation Tips

  • Study the boss order so you know when to save cooldowns and when to push
  • Bring consumables -- new raids mean new flask/food requirements
  • Set up WeakAuras early. Boss mod authors usually have encounter profiles ready within the first week
  • Have your tier set ready via the Catalyst if possible -- set bonuses matter in progression

The Bottom Line

Eight bosses, strong theming, and callbacks to WoW's troll raid legacy make the Venomous Abyss one of the most anticipated raid tiers in recent memory. The boss variety looks solid -- from multi-target golem fights to dual-boss encounters to a curse-themed final boss. Whether you're pushing Mythic progression or just trying to clear Normal in the first week, there's a lot to look forward to here.

Get your raid team organized. Season 2 is almost here.
