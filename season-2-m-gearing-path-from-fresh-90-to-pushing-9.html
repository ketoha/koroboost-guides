Season 2 M+ Gearing Path: From Fresh 90 to Pushing +9

A guide by Koroboost

You've hit 90, your gear is a mess of quest rewards and world drops, and you're staring at the Mythic+ dungeon finder wondering where to start. Here's the deal: Season 2 of Midnight has a clean, predictable gearing ladder through M+, and if you understand the breakpoints, you'll save yourself weeks of wasted effort.

The Three Tiers of Mistcrests

Season 2 uses Mistcrests as its upgrade currency, and they come in three flavors tied directly to key level:

Champion Mistcrests (M+2 to M+3)

  • Drop from every timed and untimed key in this range
  • Upgrade gear to ilvl 285-302
  • You'll burn through this tier fast -- it's basically your entry ticket

Hero Mistcrests (M+4 to M+8)

  • The meat of your gearing journey
  • Upgrade gear to ilvl 298-315
  • You'll spend the most time here, and that's fine

Myth Mistcrests (M+9 and above)

  • The endgame currency
  • Upgrade gear to ilvl 311-328 at base, with Myth 8/6 hitting 334 and 9/6 reaching 337
  • These are weekly-capped, so plan your upgrades carefully

The key insight: don't hoard crests at one tier waiting for "perfect pieces." Upgrade what you have and keep pushing. Replaced gear isn't wasted -- it got you into higher keys where better stuff drops.

Your Week-by-Week Gearing Plan

Week 1: M+2 to M+3 Sprint

Your goal is simple: get into M+2s immediately and grind out Champion Mistcrests. At this level, mechanics are forgiving and groups are plentiful. Run every dungeon in the rotation at least once -- you want to learn the routes while gear is dropping everywhere.

By the end of week 1, you should be sitting around ilvl 290-295 with most slots filled by dungeon drops or upgraded pieces.

Weeks 2-3: The M+4 to M+8 Grind

This is where you live for a while. Hero Mistcrests start dropping, and the item level jumps are meaningful. Focus on:

  • Timing keys for maximum crests -- a timed +6 gives more crests than a depleted +8
  • Running keys you know well over pushing keys that stress you out
  • Targeting specific dungeons for the drops you actually need (more on that below)

If you're struggling with certain affixes or dungeon mechanics at this tier, checking out a comprehensive gearing guide can help you identify which upgrades will have the biggest impact on your survivability and throughput.

Weeks 3+: Breaking Into +9

The +9 wall is real. Groups get pickier, mistakes get punished harder, and Myth Mistcrests are weekly-capped so every upgrade decision matters. This is where strategy overtakes raw grinding.

Upgrade Priority: What to Spend Crests On First

Not all gear slots are created equal. Here's your priority list:

Tier 1: Upgrade Immediately

  • Weapon -- always, always, always your first upgrade. A weapon upgrade is worth more DPS/healing/threat than any other single slot. Period.
  • Trinkets -- especially proc-based trinkets that scale with item level. A 15-ilvl bump on a good trinket is massive.

Tier 2: Upgrade Next

  • Rings -- secondary stats on rings are disproportionately high, and the stamina bump helps survivability
  • Helm/Chest/Legs -- big stat sticks with high stamina

Tier 3: Fill In Later

  • Shoulders/Gloves/Boots -- important but smaller stat budgets
  • Belt/Bracers/Cloak -- lowest priority, upgrade last

One common mistake: people upgrade their tier set pieces first because they feel "important." Unless the tier bonus specifically scales with ilvl (rare), your weapon upgrade is still better.

The 8-Dungeon Rotation and Targeted Farming

Season 2 runs an 8-dungeon rotation, which means you have a fixed pool of loot tables to work with. Before you start spamming keys, check what drops where:

  • Identify your 2-3 "must have" items -- check loot tables for your best-in-slot trinkets, weapons, and stat-heavy pieces
  • Spam those specific dungeons at whatever key level gives you the crests you need
  • Use your weekly vault strategically -- run at least 8 dungeons per week for maximum vault choices, and push your highest key for the best vault ilvl

The vault is your single biggest gearing tool. A +9 timed key puts Myth-track gear in your vault, which is a massive jump. Even if you can only time one +9 per week, do it.

Common Gearing Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1: Waiting for BiS before upgrading

Your "best in slot" piece from a +9 does nothing for you if you can't clear +9s yet. Upgrade your current gear to push higher, then replace it later.

Mistake 2: Ignoring secondary stats

An ilvl upgrade isn't always better if it tanks your best secondary stat. For most specs, a 5-ilvl downgrade with perfect secondaries beats a 5-ilvl upgrade with garbage stats. Check your spec's stat priority.

Mistake 3: Not running enough keys for the vault

The vault wants 1, 4, or 8 dungeons completed. Always aim for 8. Those extra vault slots dramatically increase your chances of getting usable gear.

Mistake 4: Only running keys you're comfortable with

Growth happens at the edge of your comfort zone. If you're timing +6s easily, push into +7s even if you deplete a few. The gear and experience are worth it.

When You're Stuck

Sometimes the gear plateau hits hard. You're at 305 ilvl, +7s feel rough, and upgrades aren't dropping. This is normal. Options:

  • Switch to an off-spec role -- tank and healer queues are faster, meaning more runs per hour
  • Join a consistent group -- pugging at higher keys is miserable without coordination
  • Focus on skill over gear -- learning to play mechanics perfectly at your current ilvl often matters more than 5 more item levels
  • Consider getting a boost through tough keys -- sometimes a few high-level M+ clears can break through a plateau and get you the drops or vault options you need

The Bottom Line

Season 2 M+ gearing is straightforward once you understand the system: push keys, earn crests, upgrade strategically (weapon first), and never skip your weekly vault. The path from fresh 90 to pushing +9s takes most players 3-4 weeks of consistent play. Don't rush it, don't waste crests on low-priority slots, and don't be afraid to run keys that might challenge you. That's where the good gear lives.
