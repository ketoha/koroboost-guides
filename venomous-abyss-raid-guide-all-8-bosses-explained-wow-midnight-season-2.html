Venomous Abyss Raid Guide: All 8 Bosses Explained (WoW Midnight Season 2)

The Venomous Abyss is the flagship raid of WoW Midnight Season 2, launching alongside Patch 12.1 on The Coiled Isle. Eight bosses, a full serpentine dungeon dripping in shadow and venom, and some of the most punishing mechanics the game has seen since the early Midnight cycle. This venomous abyss guide covers every encounter from pull to kill, with practical tactics you can actually use on raid night.

Whether you're leading a guild prog team or stepping in for a pug clear, understanding the full picture before you walk in saves wipes and lockouts. Let's go through every boss.

Venomous Abyss Overview

Location: The Coiled Isle, accessible via the main Midnight continent hub.

Patch: 12.1 (Season 2)

Bosses: 8

Loot: Normal 285-302 ilvl / Heroic 298-315 ilvl / Mythic 311-328 ilvl (final 2 bosses drop 337 on Mythic)

The raid is linear with a few optional branching corridors in the mid-section. The Coiled Isle itself is a tortollan archaeological site that was overrun by void-serpent entities -- so you'll fight through excavation chambers, flooded grottos, and a final sanctum that descends into the actual abyss below the island.

Boss 1: Nek'zali the Soulcoiler

The gatekeeper of Venomous Abyss. Nek'zali is a massive void serpent who coils around the entrance chamber and uses Soul Drain to siphon life from players who stand in marked zones. Interrupt rotations are critical in Phase 2 when she starts casting Venom Surge rapidly.

Key mechanics:

  • Soulcoil - a ground spiral that moves outward from the boss; walk through the gaps, don't stop

  • Venom Surge - a cast that needs to be interrupted; set up a 4-person rotation on progression

  • Soul Drain - two players get targeted, move apart and soak independently

  • Phase 2 (50%) - Nek'zali submerges and sends out echo adds; cleave them down, don't tunnel the boss

Full strategy breakdown available in our Venomous Abyss raid guide with boss-by-boss Mythic notes.

Boss 2: Entombed Sentinels

A dual-boss encounter. Two ancient tortollan constructs reanimated by shadow energy fight simultaneously. The core mechanic is managing their shared Resonance bar -- if both bosses cast their abilities within 2 seconds of each other, Resonance triggers and deals massive raid damage.

Key mechanics:

  • Phase offset tanking -- tanks must keep the two sentinels positioned at max melee range from each other

  • Shell Slam - the tank debuff; swap after 2 stacks

  • Void Burst - a soakable puddle; always soak these or they leave permanent patches

  • Resonance Pulse (Heroic+) - if both sentinels cast simultaneously, 40% max health to all players; use raid cooldowns

DPS priority: keep them within 5% health of each other through the whole fight, or the surviving one enrages.

Boss 3: Vashnik the Malignant

A corruption-bloated void creature that fills the room with spreading Malignant Pools. This is a movement and positioning fight -- ranged and healers spend most of it walking backwards while melee chase the boss.

Key mechanics:

  • Malignant Seep - Vashnik periodically leaks a pool under his current position; tanks must move the boss constantly in a slow spiral

  • Corruption Spew - targeted vomit on two random players; move away from the raid before it lands

  • Pustule Eruption - multiple orbs spawn and home in on players; run through them in a single-file line to burst all at once rather than letting them spread

  • Malignant Nova (enrage check) - if more than 8 pools exist simultaneously, Vashnik enrages; keep the room clean

Boss 4: The Lost Explorers

Three tortollan explorer spirits trapped in the abyss. This is the most mechanically dense fight in the first half of Venomous Abyss. Each explorer has a unique ability set and a shared health pool, but they take reduced damage unless all three are within 10 yards of each other.

The three explorers:

  • Cartographer Shrix - draws Ley Lines on the ground; standing on them grants a stacking haste buff to the boss, not to you

  • Diver Ozzuk - periodically dives and resurfaces under a random player; get out of the targeted zone

  • Scholar Pelyx - the caster; interrupts mandatory, casts Void Edict which silences the whole raid if it lands

Strategy: Stack all three explorers on top of each other at all times. Use a dedicated interrupter on Pelyx only. The rest of the raid focuses on Ozzuk's dive markers. Heroic adds an overlapping Ley Line phase -- pre-mark safe corridor positions before the pull.

Boss 5: Coilwarden Szeth'rak

The abyss lieutenant. Szeth'rak is a serpentine commander who summons coil-spawn adds and fights from a raised platform that rotates over a poison pit. Falling in the pit is a one-shot.

Key mechanics:

  • Platform Rotation - the platform rotates 90 degrees every 45 seconds; reorient boss positioning after each rotation

  • Venom Coil - chains two players together; they must run apart within 5 seconds to break it, or both get crushed

  • Coil-spawn adds - interrupt their Fang Flurry cast or it causes a tank one-shot

  • Poison Pit (falling) - knockback happens during Venom Coil breaks; stay near the center

Boss 6: The Abyssal Choir

A chorus of void-touched naga wraiths who sing in unison to power each other up. The fight is about silencing the right choir members at the right time to prevent their resonance stacking to lethal levels.

Key mechanics:

  • Six wraith targets -- three are "active singers" at any time, marked with a visual glow

  • Silencing the wrong wraith applies a Discordant debuff to nearby players -- coordinate carefully

  • Void Refrain - the damage-dealing ability that scales with how many active singers are alive; priority on silencing the correct targets

  • Crescendo (Phase 2 at 40%) - all six become active simultaneously; use personal defensives and 2 raid cooldowns back to back

Boss 7: Ixath the Devoured

Second-to-last boss. Ixath is a player-turned-monstrosity, a former adventurer consumed by the abyss. This fight has a strong personal responsibility component -- several mechanics are fail states for the individual, not the raid.

Key mechanics:

  • Abyss Mark - targets a player; if they don't reach the designated soak zone in 8 seconds, they transform into an add

  • Devour - tank mechanic; each hit applies Consumed, a stacking healing absorption debuff; swap at 4 stacks

  • Phantom Relapse - Ixath re-enacts abilities from the first 3 bosses in sequence during Phase 2; know your Nek'zali, Vashnik, and Entombed Sentinels mechanics cold

  • Mythic: two players get Abyss Mark simultaneously with different zone positions

Boss 8: Ula'tek (Final Boss)

The ruler of Venomous Abyss. Ula'tek is an ancient serpent god sealed beneath the Coiled Isle by the tortollans centuries ago. The fight has three phases and a full room transition between Phase 2 and 3.

Phase 1 (100-70%):

  • Soulvenom Cascade - a wave of poison that sweeps across the room; use the terrain gaps to avoid

  • Coiling Doom - wraps around a player, rooting them; nearby players must break the coil by dealing damage to it within 6 seconds

  • Void Geyser - soakable; 2-3 players per geyser, more than 4 causes Overload which knocks everyone away

Phase 2 (70-40%):

  • Room splits into four quadrants; each quadrant has a Venom Font that needs to be destroyed before Ula'tek's energy bar fills

  • Assign 5-player kill squads to each quadrant; tanks hold the boss in the center

  • Shadow Hunger - if the energy bar fills, Ula'tek heals to 55%; the fight becomes a DPS check

Phase 3 (40-0%):

  • The room floods with shadow; only raised platforms are safe

  • Ula'tek gains Abyssal Fury -- all abilities happen 30% faster

  • Enrage at 9 minutes from pull; DPS check is real on Mythic

  • Final 10%: Soulvenom Cascade becomes a full-room wipe mechanic except for one narrow safe lane; pre-position for this

Tips for Prog Week

  • Clear Normal first for Catalyst charges and the full boss walkthrough -- mechanics carry over to Heroic with additions

  • Bosses 4 (The Lost Explorers) and 7 (Ixath) are the main roadblocks for most guilds on Heroic

  • Ula'tek Phase 3 is the Mythic prog wall -- expect 100+ pulls from serious teams on week 1

  • Bring an interrupt rotation spreadsheet for Nek'zali and Abyssal Choir; both fights punish uncoordinated interrupts hard

  • The venomous abyss guide you use should specify tank positioning diagrams for Vashnik -- the pool management is the part most raid guides undersell
