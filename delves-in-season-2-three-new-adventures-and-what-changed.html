Delves in Season 2: Three New Adventures and What Changed

A guide by Koroboost

Delves were the surprise hit of Midnight's launch -- bite-sized dungeon content you could solo or run with a small group, no queue anxiety, no toxic pugs. Season 2 expands the system with three new delves, story variants for existing ones, and tweaked rewards that make them a legitimate gearing path alongside M+ and raids.

The Three New Delves

Ring of Glory

A gladiatorial arena-style delve set in an underground fighting pit. You're working through waves of combatants with mini-boss encounters between rounds. The vibe is very different from the exploration-focused delves of Season 1.

What makes it stand out:

  • Wave-based structure means consistent pacing -- no wandering around looking for objectives
  • Champion enemies spawn as optional challenges during waves for bonus loot
  • The final boss adapts based on how quickly you cleared waves, adding a soft enrage mechanic

Ring of Glory is one of the faster delves to complete, making it popular for farming.

Gnarldor Isle

An outdoor coastal delve with verticality. You're navigating cliff paths, underwater caves, and a corrupted treant grove. This one leans into exploration and has more optional content than the others.

Key features:

  • Multiple paths to the final objective, each with different difficulty and rewards
  • Underwater sections with limited air supply (bringing consumables helps)
  • Hidden treasure chests scattered throughout that drop bonus crests

Gnarldor Isle rewards players who explore rather than speedrun. If you like the "adventure" part of delves, this is your favorite.

Venomfall Deeps (Nemesis Delve)

The flagship addition. Venomfall Deeps is Season 2's Nemesis Delve -- a higher-difficulty delve designed to be the pinnacle of solo content. Think of it as a delve tuned closer to a mythic dungeon.

What makes Nemesis Delves different:

  • Harder baseline difficulty across all tiers
  • Unique mechanics that require actual strategy, not just gear
  • Exclusive cosmetic rewards (mount, transmog set) only available from this delve
  • Leaderboards tracking fastest clear times by class/spec

Venomfall Deeps is themed around the venom/snake motif of Season 2, with poison mechanics, visibility-reducing fog, and enemies that apply stacking DoTs. It's genuinely challenging at higher tiers and represents Blizzard's answer to "delves are too easy."

For a full breakdown of strategies and tier recommendations for all three new delves, the Midnight delves guide covers everything you need.

Existing Delves Get Snake/Venom Story Variants

Season 1's delves aren't going away -- they're getting story variants tied to the Season 2 narrative. The Arathi venom cult storyline weaves through existing delves, changing enemy compositions and adding new objectives.

What this means practically:

  • New enemy types in familiar layouts -- venom-infused versions of existing mobs
  • Modified boss encounters with poison-themed mechanics layered on top
  • Updated loot tables reflecting Season 2 itemization
  • New voice lines and story beats connecting to the seasonal narrative

It's not a complete overhaul, but it keeps the existing delves feeling fresh rather than stale.

Bountiful Delves in Season 2

Bountiful Delves are back. These are enhanced versions of regular delves that appear on a rotation, offering significantly better rewards for a single weekly lockout per delve.

Season 2 Bountiful Delves:

  • Available from the first week of the season
  • Rotate daily -- check your map for the golden delve icon
  • Drop extra crests and have higher chances at rare items
  • Include the three new delves in the rotation

If you're limited on play time, always prioritize your Bountiful Delve runs for the week. The reward-per-minute ratio is unbeatable.

Crest Rewards by Tier

Here's what you're earning at each delve tier:

| Tier | Crest Type | Notes |

|------|-----------|-------|

| T1-T3 | No crests | Learning tiers, gear drops only |

| T4 | Adventurer Mistcrests | Entry-level crests, baseline upgrades |

| T5-T6 | Veteran Mistcrests | Solid midrange, good for alts |

| T7-T10 | Champion Mistcrests | Meaningful upgrades, main character territory |

| T11 | Hero Mistcrests | Endgame-tier crests from delves |

The big change from Season 1: T11 now drops Hero Mistcrests, which was previously locked behind M+4 or higher. This makes delves a real alternative to M+ for gearing, not just a stepping stone.

Note that Myth Mistcrests are still M+9/raid exclusive. Delves won't get you to absolute BiS, but they'll get you 90% of the way there.

Solo vs Group Scaling

Delves scale based on group size (1-4 players), and Season 2 tightens this scaling:

  • Solo: Brann (your NPC companion) has been buffed. He's smarter about interrupts and positioning, and his talent tree gets new options in Season 2.
  • 2 players: Considered the sweet spot. Content scales moderately, but having a real person for CC and interrupts makes higher tiers much smoother.
  • 3-4 players: Scales significantly harder. Designed for groups that want dungeon-level coordination without the M+ timer pressure.

For solo players, investing in Brann's talent tree is crucial. The difference between a poorly specced Brann and an optimized one is night and day at T8+.

Should You Do Delves for Gear?

Yes, especially if:

  • You prefer solo or small-group content
  • You don't enjoy the M+ timer pressure
  • You're gearing alts and want something chill
  • You want Hero Mistcrests without dealing with pugs

Stick to M+ if:

  • You want Myth Mistcrests (M+9+ exclusive)
  • You enjoy the competitive timed format
  • You need specific dungeon drops

The ideal approach is both. Run delves for consistent crest income and M+ for the ceiling. If you're looking to fast-track your delve progression or push into higher tiers, delve boost services can save you a lot of time on alts.

Quick Start for Season 2 Delves

  • Run all three new delves at T1 first to learn mechanics
  • Push Venomfall Deeps specifically for the Nemesis cosmetics
  • Hit your Bountiful Delves every week without fail
  • Spec Brann for your playstyle before pushing above T8
  • Farm T11 for Hero Mistcrests once you're comfortable

Season 2 delves are a genuine endgame path now, not just a casual side activity. Take them seriously and they'll reward you.
